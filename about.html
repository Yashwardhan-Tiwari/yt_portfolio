A quick introduction

I’m early in the journey — and intentional about it.

I’m a first-year student in the Faculty of Engineering and Technology (FET), CSE department, at South Asian University. I’m learning programming by practising the basics, experimenting with the browser and slowly taking on projects that require me to connect multiple concepts.

Right now, my core toolkit includes C, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I’m also learning Python. Outside languages, I’m interested in optimisation, linear programming and the way a well-designed system can make a messy real-world process simpler.

I use VS Code, Git and GitHub, and work across Windows and Ubuntu Linux. I’m not trying to present myself as an expert — I’m here to build a strong foundation and keep improving.
